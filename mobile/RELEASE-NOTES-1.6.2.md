# One Roof 1.6.2 — What's New

## App Store (English, U.S.)

Bingo night, and a smarter Roof:

• New game: Bingo! Open a room, everyone gets their own card, the cage spins and drops a ball. Mark your numbers and shout BINGO before anyone else.
• Roof asks how long an event lasts, and warns you when it clashes with something already on the calendar.
• "Open this month's budget" takes you straight there.
• Roof answers with its voice sooner.
• Pets: Roof tells you what it just marked and what was already done.
• Lists: "add it to the general list" now works.
• A clearer warning when location sharing needs "Always".
• Plus fixes and polish.
