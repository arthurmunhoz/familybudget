# One Roof: store listing in Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish (Mexico)

Until now both stores had only the English (U.S.) listing, so people in Brazil
and Latin America read English in search even though the app is in their
language (owner, 2026-11-19). This is the 1.6.x English listing
(`STORE-LISTING-1.6.0.md`), written for each language, not translated word for
word. Claims follow the same rules as the English text; on Play, Whereabouts,
widgets, Apple Calendar and Face ID stay out. The wake phrase is per
language: pt "Oi Casa", es "Oye Casa".

Store locales: App Store "Portuguese (Brazil)" and "Spanish (Mexico)"; Google
Play "pt-BR" and "es-419". Spanish (Mexico) on the App Store also serves the
rest of Latin America.

Character counts are checked by `scripts/listing-lengths.py`.
