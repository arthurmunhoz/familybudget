# One Roof 1.6.0 — What's New

## App Store (English, U.S.)

Meet Roof, your family's voice assistant — and a lot more:

• Roof: say "Hey Roof" and ask. Add to lists, log an expense, check the calendar, move "Sam's dentist", log the dog's vet visit, find a document, start a game. It keeps listening until you say thanks.
• Scan a receipt and Roof spots what you bought from your lists — tick it all off in one tap.
• Today's cards on the Home now show what each thing is: the vet with your pet's photo, birthdays, school, sports and more.
• Family games: Hangman (challenge someone and see who wins), Battleship and a buzzer quiz — each person on their own phone.
• Modo Casa: any device with internet becomes your family's home hub — agenda, lists, Roof and the photo frame on one screen. Now right on the Home.
• Lists: any list, not just shopping — chores, a trip, school.
• Several families on one account.
• Documents: a new vault door, and PDF previews.
• Pets: routine times put the day in order.
• Money: an icon for each budget and lighter cards.
• Export your family's data any time.
• Plus many fixes and polish.
