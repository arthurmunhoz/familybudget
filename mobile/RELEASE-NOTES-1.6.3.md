# One Roof 1.6.3 — What's New

Kept high-level (owner's rule since 1.6.2): the headline, then "fixes".

## App Store (English, U.S.)

A better family calendar:

• Reminders when you want them: at the start, 5 or 10 minutes before, an hour, a day, a week.
• "Family today" on Home: everyone's day in one line, one tap to see it all.
• Day view: pick whose events to show, full titles on short events, and what's over fades away.
• Google Calendar events now show who's invited and the meeting link.
• Fixes and polish.
