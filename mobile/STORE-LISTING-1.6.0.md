# One Roof 1.6.0: store listing text

1.6.0 is the biggest release since launch: Roof (the voice assistant), Games,
Modo Casa, multiple families, and lists for anything. The old listing still
sells v1.2 (Whereabouts, receipt scan), so the text below replaces it. Both
stores are English (U.S.) only. The What's New notes are in
`RELEASE-NOTES-1.6.0.md`.

Every claim here comes from the app as it is in 1.6.0. On Play, Whereabouts,
widgets, Apple Calendar and Face ID stay out (`PLAY-STORE-RELEASE.md` §0b, §4).

```
Run your whole home from one app — not five. And now, just ask.

One Roof brings your family's calendar, lists, budget, pets, documents and everyday coordination into one shared space. Everyone at home sees the same thing, in sync, instantly.

NEW: ROOF, YOUR FAMILY'S ASSISTANT
Say "Hey Roof" (or tap the orb) and ask in your own words: "milk and eggs on the list", "I spent 50 at the supermarket", "what's on tomorrow?", "move the dentist to Monday", "tell everyone dinner's ready". Roof does it and shows you what changed, with Undo. It keeps listening until you're done.

• LISTS — groceries by store, chores, a trip, school. Add an item and it appears on everyone's phone; tick things off as you go.

• UTILITIES — split a bill (even by item, from a photo), find the better deal, work out a discount, print luggage tags, and turn a spare device into a family photo frame.
• MODO CASA — a spare tablet or laptop becomes the family's home screen: agenda, lists, money and photos at a glance.
• WIDGETS — today's agenda, budget and nudges on your Home Screen.

BUILT FOR REAL FAMILIES
• One household, one bill: invite everyone, no per-person charge. Several families on one account.
• Private by design: your data is visible only to your household. No ads. No tracking.
• English, Spanish and Portuguese.

One roof. One app. Everything your household needs to run smoothly.
```
