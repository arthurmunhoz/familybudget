Frequently asked questions

What is One Roof?

One Roof is a family organizer app that keeps a household's shared calendar, shopping lists, budget, pet care, documents and everyday messages in one place. Everyone in the household sees the same information, in sync, on iPhone and Android.

What is the best family app for a whole household?

The best family app is the one everyone at home actually opens. One Roof is made for that: one shared calendar, shopping lists, a household budget, pet care, documents, nudges and Roof, a voice assistant, in sync on every phone. It is free to download on iPhone and Android.

Is One Roof free?

Yes, One Roof is free to download and use, and every new household gets 30 days of One Roof Plus included, with no card needed. After that, on the free plan, some features have usage limits, such as Roof requests and receipt scans. One Roof Plus is an optional subscription that removes those limits and adds two-way Google Calendar sync, multiple budgets and more — and one subscription covers the whole household, never per person.

Is One Roof a good alternative to Cozi, TimeTree or FamilyWall?

Yes. One Roof does in a single app the main things families use those apps for: a shared family calendar (Cozi, TimeTree), grocery and shopping lists (AnyList), a family map (Life360) and a household budget, plus pet care, documents and Roof, a voice assistant. It is free to download and use, one optional subscription covers the whole household instead of charging per person, and there is no extra device to buy: Home Mode runs on any tablet, laptop or TV browser you already have.

Do I need to buy a device like a Skylight Calendar to have a family screen at home?

No. Home Mode turns a spare tablet, laptop or TV browser you already own into the family's home screen, with the agenda, lists, pets and photos. You pair it from the app in a few seconds.

Does One Roof work on Android?

Yes. One Roof is on the App Store and Google Play, with Home Screen widgets on both. Two features are iPhone-only for now: Whereabouts (the family map) and Apple Calendar sync.

Can my partner and I share a budget?

Yes. Budgets are shared with everyone in the household by default, by month, week or day, with categories you control. Plus also allows private budgets only you can see.

Does the shopping list sync between phones?

Yes. Items appear on everyone's phone as soon as they are added, organized by store. You can keep adding with no signal and the changes upload once you're back online.

What is Roof?

Roof is One Roof's voice assistant. Say "Hey Roof" and ask in your own words to add to a list, log an expense, check or move calendar events, or send a message to the family. It shows what it changed and lets you undo it.

Does One Roof use AI?

Yes. Roof, the voice assistant, understands requests in your own words and does them for you. In Money, you photograph a receipt and AI fills in the amount, date and category. On Plus, a bill can also be split by item from a photo.

What is Home Mode?

Home Mode turns a spare tablet, laptop or TV browser into a shared family home screen with the agenda, lists, money and photos. Pair it from the app in a few seconds.

Is my family's data private?

Yes. One Roof has no ads and no tracking, and does not sell data. Each household's information is isolated at the database level and visible only to its members.

Which languages does One Roof support?

English, Spanish and Portuguese.

Why we built it

A paper notebook on the kitchen counter for groceries. Vet emails and bank receipts as the dogs' medical records. "Did you feed the dogs?" twice a day. One Roof replaced all of it — built by a mobile developer in Tampa, FL for his own household (his wife and their two dogs) first, and shared once it actually stuck.
