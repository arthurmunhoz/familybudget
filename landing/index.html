Your family's data is not the product

No ads. No tracking SDKs. No selling location data. One Roof exists because we use it under our own roof — your household's budgets, documents, and locations stay yours, protected per-household at the database level.

Why we built it

A shared notebook on the kitchen counter for groceries. Vet emails and bank receipts as the dogs' medical records. "Did you feed the dogs today?" — twice a day, every day. A savings goal that never survived the month. One Roof replaced all of it — built by a mobile developer for his own household first, and shared once it actually stuck.
