Your family's data is not the product

No ads. No tracking SDKs. No selling location data. One Roof exists because we use it under our own roof — your household's budgets, documents, and locations stay yours, protected per-household at the database level.

On Android? Install from your browser

One Roof runs as a full app on Android — no store needed, same household, same live sync as iPhone.

  1. Open one-roof-app.vercel.app in Chrome
  2. Sign in and tap Install app when prompted (or Menu → Add to Home screen)
  3. That's it — it's on your home screen like any other app
