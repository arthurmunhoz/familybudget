Questions

Is One Roof a good FamilyWall alternative?

Yes. Like FamilyWall, One Roof keeps the family calendar, lists, budget, location and documents in one app, and it adds Roof (a voice assistant), receipt scanning, pet care, family games and Home Mode. One Roof has no meal planner and no family chat, so keep that in mind if you use those in FamilyWall.

Does One Roof have a family locator?

Yes, on iPhone: Whereabouts is a private family map with live locations and arrive/leave alerts for places you choose. It is off by default and visible only to your household. It is not available on Android yet.

How much does One Roof cost?

One Roof is free to download and use, and every new household gets 30 days of One Roof Plus included, with no card needed. After that, Plus is an optional subscription that covers the whole household, never per person.

Does One Roof have ads?

No. One Roof has no ads and no tracking, and does not sell data.
